## 3. How Veralux Works

Veralux builds a capsule wardrobe from the measurements, colours, fabrics, patterns, fashion houses and budget that you enter. You are in control of every step:

* **Capsule.** A capsule contains 36 to 44 pieces, built from the Paris (FHCM) or Italy (CNMI) wardrobe philosophy that you choose. A rules-based solver selects the pieces, and an independent checker re-verifies that the result meets the rules and your budget. If no combination works, the App tells you why and offers the smallest change that would help instead of showing a partial capsule.
* **Swap, approve, rebuild.** You can swap any piece, approve the ones you want to keep, or edit your inputs and rebuild.
* **Buy.** You can buy the whole capsule or only the pieces you choose. Nothing is bought until you review your cart, enter your delivery and payment details, and confirm.

The capsule is a recommendation. You decide what to buy.
