# Privacy Policy for Veralux

**Effective Date:** October 3, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Veralux ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you download, install, or use our mobile application, **Veralux** (the "Application" or "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By installing or using Veralux, you agree to the terms of this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not install or use the Application.

**The short version:** Veralux has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your body measurements and style preferences stay on your device. Your contact and delivery details are shared only when you choose to place an order, and only with the companies that need them to deliver it.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Veralux builds your capsule wardrobe on your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or attribution software development kits (SDKs). The App does not read your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and does not send you push notifications.

### A. Information We Do NOT Collect

* **Advertising or Tracking Identifiers:** We do not collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID), and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The App does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track.
* **Location Data:** We do not access or collect location data of any kind.
* **Usage Analytics or Diagnostics:** We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or performance telemetry. Timing information the App records for development is written only to a local log on your device and is not transmitted.
* **Camera, Microphone, Photos or Contacts:** The App does not request access to any of these.
* **Body Measurements and Style Preferences:** These never leave your device (see Section 1(B)).

### B. Information Stored Locally, On Your Device Only

To provide its core function, Veralux stores the following information **on your device**:

* **Your measurements:** bust or chest, waist, hips, and, if you enter them, height, inseam and US shoe size, plus your chosen unit (centimetres or inches).
* **Your style profile:** your wardrobe (women's or men's), your style approach (Paris or Italy), and the colours, fabrics, patterns, silhouettes and fashion houses you select.
* **Your budget.**
* **Fit adjustments:** a small note, by garment type, of whether a returned item was too small or too large, so the App can refine future sizing.
* **Your capsule, cart and approvals:** the pieces in your current capsule, the ones you have approved, and the items in your cart.
* **Your order history ("Transaction History"):** the orders you have placed and their status.
* **Your saved delivery details:** your name, delivery address, phone number and email address. These are kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore, through `expo-secure-store`).

Your measurements and style profile are used on your device to select sizes and build your capsule. They are **not** sent to us or to any supplier. When you order, only the **size label** of each piece you chose (for example "IT 40" or "M") is sent with the order, not your measurements.

### C. Information Shared When You Place an Order

If you buy pieces through the App, we and the companies that complete your order need the following information:

* **Order details:** the pieces, sizes and quantities you buy, the prices and the order total, including tax.
* **Delivery and contact details:** your name, delivery address, phone number and email address. A carrier needs your phone number and email address to deliver an international parcel and to send tracking updates.
* **Payment details:** your card number, expiry date and security code are entered into the App's payment form and sent directly to our payment processor, **Stripe**. They are held in memory while the form is open, cleared after payment, and are never saved on your device or on our servers. We receive only a payment confirmation and a payment reference.

Your order details and delivery details are processed on our servers so that we can place your order with the supplier, collect and show you tracking information, and handle returns and support. We use this information for no other purpose.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information in Section 1 only to:

* Build your capsule and choose pieces in your size, colours and budget, on your device.
* Show your cart, orders and tracking in one place.
* Take payment, place your order with the supplier that carries each piece, and deliver it.
* Tell you if an item has sold out and help you replace it.
* Process returns and refunds, and answer your support requests.
* Meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations, and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you.

---

## 3. Who We Share Information With

We share order information only with the companies that need it to complete an order you place:

* **Fashion wholesale suppliers** (currently **Brands Gateway** and **Nokin Style**, and any similar supplier we add) receive your order lines, delivery address, phone number and email address so they can pack and ship your items. Some suppliers ship from the European Union and the United States, so your information may be transferred across borders.
* **Shipping carriers** used by the suppliers (for example DHL, FedEx and UPS) receive your delivery details to deliver the parcel.
* **Stripe**, our payment processor, receives your payment details and billing information, and handles them under its own privacy policy.
* **Tax and fraud-prevention providers**, if we use them, receive only what is needed to calculate tax or review a payment.
* **Authorities and advisers**, where the law requires it or where we need advice to protect our rights.

Each of these companies handles your information under its own policy and legal obligations. We require suppliers to use your details only to fulfil your order.

### Amazon

Some pieces are sold by Amazon. For these, the App opens Amazon, and you buy from Amazon directly with your Amazon account. We do not take payment for these items and do not send your delivery details to Amazon. Amazon's privacy notice governs your purchase. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, which means Amazon may tell us that a purchase was made through our link, without telling us who you are.

## 4. Third-Party Websites, Images and the In-App Browser

* **Fashion house websites.** The Home tab lists fashion houses. When you tap one, its website opens in an in-app browser sheet (Safari View Controller on iOS or Chrome Custom Tabs on Android). That site is run by the fashion house, not by us. We cannot see what you do there, and the site may set cookies or collect information under its own policy.
* **Product images.** Product photographs are loaded over HTTPS from our suppliers' image servers and saved in a cache on your device so they load faster. When your device requests an image, the server that hosts it can see your device's IP address and user-agent string, as any website would. We do not receive this information.
* **Links.** We do not embed third-party tracking tools, advertising or social-media plug-ins in the App.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Veralux is not directed to children. Purchases require you to be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). If you believe a child has placed an order or sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

* **On your device:** Your measurements, preferences, capsule, cart, order history and saved delivery details stay on your device until you delete them. Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete my data** to erase your profile, settings, order history and saved address from the device and reset the App. Deleting the App also removes its stored data, per standard iOS and Android behavior. Cached product images are removed when the App is deleted or your device clears the cache.
* **Order records we hold:** We keep order, delivery, payment-reference and return records for as long as needed to deliver your order, handle returns, refunds and complaints, and meet tax, accounting and legal requirements. After that we delete or anonymise them.
* **Requesting deletion of order records:** Email us at the address in Section 10 and we will delete the order information we hold about you, except what we are required by law to keep. The in-app "Delete my data" button cannot remove these server-side records on its own.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to know what we hold, to access, correct or delete it, to receive a copy, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Because most of your information lives only on your device, you control it directly through the App and your device settings. For the order records we hold, email us and we will respond within the time the law allows (30 days in the EEA and UK, 45 days in California). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

**Legal bases (EEA and UK):** We process order and delivery information because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (to deliver what you bought), to comply with legal obligations (tax and accounting), and for our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and keeping our service secure. **California:** We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

---

## 8. Security

Your delivery details are kept in your device's secure storage. Your other App data is held in the App's private storage, which your device protects with its passcode, biometrics and disk encryption. Information sent between the App and our servers, and card details sent to Stripe, travel over encrypted HTTPS connections. We limit the order information on our servers to what is needed to fulfil orders. No system is completely secure, so we recommend that you keep your device and its operating system up to date and protected with a passcode.
